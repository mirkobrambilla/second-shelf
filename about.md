title: About
---

Second Shelf is a collection of **technical notes** from home projects, experiments, and whatever hardware I managed to keep alive a bit longer.

This is not enterprise work, and it doesn’t pretend to be.
There are no SLAs, no perfect architectures, and definitely no polished deliverables waiting at the end.
Most of what you’ll find here is built at home, in between other responsibilities, with limited time, budget, and attention. Things break, plans change, and sometimes projects just stop halfway through.

**And that’s fine.**

Some projects are messy. Some are incomplete. Some are just quick attempts to see if something works. But everything is documented with one goal: to make it easier to pick things back up later, without having to start from scratch.

This is also a place for **reused hardware**, old devices, repurposed components, things that still have some life left in them. Not everything needs to be new to be useful.

If something works well enough, it’s good enough.
